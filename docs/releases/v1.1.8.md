# Victron VE.Bus (MK3) 1.1.8

## Fixed

- **Solar & wind priority** (switch) and **Solar & wind priority voltage** (number and
  sensor) were missing on a MultiPlus-II while the guided dialog offered them. The
  Multi doesn't report which flag bits it supports, and the entities needed that, or
  the feature switched on. They are now also created when the Multi reports a range for
  the priority voltage, which the dialog already relied on.
- The ranges of the flag settings (PowerAssist, weak AC input, UPS function, solar &
  wind priority) are read again at start-up when they come back incomplete. This is a
  read only.

Switching solar & wind priority from the dashboard is a RAM-only change, like the
other switches, and doesn't need *Allow critical changes*.
