# Victron VE.Bus (MK3) 1.1.0

Every control can now be used inside Home Assistant itself: on the device page, on
dashboards, in automations, scripts and with voice assistants. Before, the critical
controls were only in *Configure → Device actions*.

> [!CAUTION]
> Use at your own risk. The authors and contributors take no responsibility whatsoever
> for any damage. Critical controls can cut the power on AC out 1 and AC out 2, restart
> the Multi and change battery charge voltages. If you are not sure what a setting
> does, do not change it.

## New

- **Critical controls as entities** (with *Monitor and control*): a **Mode** select,
  **Passthru**, **PowerAssist**, **Weak AC input** and **UPS function** switches,
  **Absorption voltage** and **Float voltage** numbers, and **Start equalisation**,
  **Restart the Multi** and **Save settings permanently** buttons.
- **Allow critical changes**: a switch that unlocks the critical controls for 10
  minutes and then turns itself off. While it is off, a critical control refuses the
  change and says what could happen. Routine controls (current limits, charge current,
  force absorption / float, ESS) are not locked.
- **RAM first, then save**: entity changes go to the Multi's RAM and are lost when it
  restarts. **Unsaved setting changes** counts them; **Save settings permanently**
  stores them in the EEPROM.
- **Actions for automations and scripts**: `victron_vebus.set_mode`, `set_setting`,
  `set_feature`, `equalise`, `restart` and `save_settings`, each with
  `acknowledge_risk: true`.
- The guided *Configure → Device actions* dialog stays, with full explanations.

## Fixed

- Changing the mode while in passthru no longer leaves passthru. The Multi can report
  its minimum current limit instead of 0 A, so passthru is now remembered.

## Upgrading

Nothing to do. If you used 0.4 or older, the mode select, the PowerAssist / weak AC /
UPS switches, the charge voltage numbers and the equalise button come back with their
old entity IDs, now behind *Allow critical changes*.

See [Controlling the Multi](https://github.com/tubalainen/victron-vebus-hacs/blob/main/docs/controls.md)
in the documentation.
