# Victron VE.Bus (MK3) 1.1.1

A text-only release: clearer warnings about what a restart of the Multi does.

> [!CAUTION]
> When the Multi restarts, its relays open and **every device connected to AC out 1 and
> AC out 2 is switched off and on again (power cycled)**.

## Changed

- Every warning that mentions a restart now says that it power cycles every device on
  AC out 1 and AC out 2: the *Restart the Multi* dialog, button and action, the note
  that some settings may only take full effect after a restart, and the documentation.
- **Turning off battery operational limits** asks you to restart the Multi within 5
  minutes. It now warns that this power cycles the AC outputs, so you can pick a
  convenient moment.
- The **Monitor and control** warning shown when adding or reconfiguring the
  integration described 0.5 (critical changes only in *Configure → Device actions*).
  It now describes the *Allow critical changes* lock, the guided dialog and the actions.

No code or behaviour changes.
